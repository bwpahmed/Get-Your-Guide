Booking Policies

These general policies explain the booking process. The selected package, operator confirmation and special-event terms will take priority where they differ.

Booking & Payment

  • A booking is confirmed only after availability, final price, vessel or activity, date, time and payment requirement are confirmed.
  • Displayed prices may be starting prices and can change for weekends, holidays, special events, private charters or operator updates.
  • Customers should verify the selected package, adult and child count, deck or seating choice, pickup option and add-ons before payment.
  • Payment instructions are sent through the confirmed business contact. Card details are not collected through the public enquiry form.

Confirmation Delivery

  • The final voucher, booking reference, boarding point, pickup details and contact number are sent by WhatsApp or email.
  • Customers should keep the confirmation available offline and arrive within the stated check-in window.
  • Unconfirmed screenshots, draft enquiries or unpaid requests do not count as final booking confirmation.

Cancellation & Refund

  • The cancellation deadline depends on the selected operator and package. The applicable deadline is shared before payment.
  • Late cancellations, no-shows, missed boarding, incorrect pickup details or arrival after gate closure may be non-refundable.
  • Private charters, New Year, public holidays and specially arranged services may use stricter or non-refundable terms.
  • If an operator cancels and no suitable replacement is accepted, the refund process will follow the confirmed payment method and operator policy.

Route, Timing & Operational Changes

  • Routes, boarding gates, pickup windows, entertainment and sailing times can change due to weather, traffic, marine instructions or operational requirements.
  • A reasonable replacement vessel or timing may be offered where necessary. Material changes will be communicated through the booking contact.

Privacy Policy

Contact and booking information is used to answer enquiries, confirm availability, arrange the selected service and provide customer support. Payment card information is not collected through the website enquiry forms. Information may be shared with the confirmed operator only where necessary to fulfil the booking.

Website Terms

Package descriptions, prices, images and availability are provided for comparison and enquiry purposes. Final operator confirmation controls the booking. Customers are responsible for reviewing the final voucher, guest details, safety restrictions, child policy and cancellation terms before travel.
