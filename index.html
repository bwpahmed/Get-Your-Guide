Frequently asked questions

Before You Book

Which Dubai cruise location should I choose?

Choose Canal for modern waterfront and Festival City views, Marina for JBR and Bluewaters skyline, and Creek only after the exact heritage route and boarding point are confirmed.

Are the prices final?

The displayed price is the package baseline. Special dates, transfer, upper-deck guarantee, celebrations or private arrangements may change the total. The final amount is confirmed before payment.

Can I choose upper-deck seating?

Some packages offer best-available seating while others allow a guaranteed upper-deck upgrade. Check the selected package and booking form.

Are children free?

Infants below three years are commonly free on shared cruises, while children aged three to eleven may use a child rate. The selected operator policy is confirmed before booking.

Where is the exact boarding point?

The final Google Maps pin, gate, contact person and check-in time are shared after the booking is confirmed.

Do you offer group discounts?

Group pricing may be available for companies, schools, tour groups, families and events. It depends on date, guest count, vessel and package.
